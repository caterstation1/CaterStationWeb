# Concept definitions

## Concept 1 — The Corporate Ordering Machine
Target: office managers, EAs and workplace organisers who need a low-risk order fast. Conversion strategy: city, occasion and headcount first, then product cards and quote threshold. Visual strategy: structured but bold with pink/yellow actions, thick borders and business confidence strips. Interactions: city switch, mobile menu, headcount slider, category filters, quick-view toast, quote modal and sticky mobile CTA. It differs by prioritising decision clarity over storytelling.

## Concept 2 — The Big Food Reveal
Target: brand-led buyers and teams planning memorable internal celebrations. Conversion strategy: create desire through a scroll story, then reveal products and support with practical objections. Visual strategy: cardboard, dark stage, oversized Impact typography, high-contrast editorial panels and theatrical product reveals. Interactions: city switch, anchored navigation, product quick-view feedback and quote modal. It differs by making the opening of the box the central experience.

## Concept 3 — Build Your Feed
Target: buyers who do not know what to order and want recommendations without talking to sales. Conversion strategy: lightweight guided recommender, then flexible browsing by craving or group size. Visual strategy: friendly interactive dashboard with fresh green, pink and strong black outlines. Interactions: city, headcount, occasion and dietary toggles produce simulated recommendations; filters and product quick views support browsing. It differs by turning product discovery into a tool.
